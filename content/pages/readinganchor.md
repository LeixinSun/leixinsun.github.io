This page describes how the **Reading Anchors** browser extension handles data.

## Summary

Reading Anchors is designed to work **locally in your browser**.

- It processes the text of the current page only after you activate the extension.
- It stores your extension settings locally in the browser.
- It does **not** send page text to our servers.
- It does **not** sell personal data.
- It does **not** use your browsing content for advertising.

## What The Extension Does

Reading Anchors adds sparse typographic emphasis to selected words and phrases on English web pages to help readers identify structure and meaning more quickly.

When you click the extension action, the extension may:

- inspect visible text on the current page
- identify useful structural or high-information reading anchors
- inject local styling into the current tab
- store your selected settings such as density and mode

## Information Processed

The extension may process the following information **locally on your device**:

- visible text content from the active web page
- page title or heading text used to improve anchor selection
- your chosen extension settings

This processing happens inside the browser context of the page and is used only to provide the extension's functionality.

## Information Stored

Reading Anchors stores limited settings in browser local storage, such as:

- whether you prefer low, medium, or high density
- whether you prefer structure, balanced, or dense mode

These settings are stored locally through browser extension storage APIs and are not intended to identify you personally.

## Information Not Collected

Reading Anchors does **not** intentionally collect or transmit:

- account passwords
- payment information
- personal profile information
- private messages
- remote copies of page text for analytics or model training

## Data Sharing

Reading Anchors does **not** share page text or reading content with third parties as part of its normal operation.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not rely on remote text-processing services to perform its reading-anchor feature.

If this changes in a future release, this policy should be updated before that release is distributed.

## Your Choices

You can:

- stop processing by turning the extension off
- remove the visual effect from the current page
- uninstall the extension at any time
- clear locally stored extension settings through your browser

## Children

Reading Anchors is not specifically directed to children under 13.

## Changes To This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's behavior changes. Material changes should be reflected on this page before or together with the relevant release.
